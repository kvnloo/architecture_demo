## PROMPT USAGE NOTES:

**For Microbot Accuracy:** Always describe the microbots as "tetrahedral" or "four-faced pyramid" shapes with "triangular magnetic connector faces." This ensures the AI generates the correct geometric form instead of generic spheres or cubes.

**Technical Style Modifiers:** Include terms like "CAD rendering," "orthographic illustration," "blueprint style," "technical line art," and "engineering documentation" to maintain professional portfolio quality.

**Background Consistency:** Specify "white background" or "clean background" for integration into portfolio documents.

**Detail Level:** Add "high-detail," "numbered parts," "dimension lines," and "material callouts" to enhance technical credibility.

**View Standards:** Explicitly request "orthographic views," "exploded assembly," "cross-section," or "isometric" to match engineering drawing conventions.
